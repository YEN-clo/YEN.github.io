碩士論文：用數據讀懂使用者偏好Thesis: reading user preference with data

以《英雄聯盟》145 位角色、974 款造型為樣本，依顏色、原型、性格分類後進行問卷調查，用三因子變異數分析找出不同性別、年齡、教育程度玩家的造型偏好差異。這段訓練讓我習慣先問「使用者到底喜歡什麼」，再動手畫。Sampled 974 skins across 145 League of Legends characters, classified by color, form and personality, surveyed players, and used three-way ANOVA to find how gender, age and education shape style preference. It taught me to ask what users actually want before I draw.
